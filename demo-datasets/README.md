# Demo Datasets

A document the starter corpus does not contain, used in the video demo to
show the pipeline handling an unseen PDF.

- `rbi-mpc-2025-04-09.pdf` — Reserve Bank of India, *Monetary
  Policy Statement 2025-26 — Resolution of the Monetary Policy Committee,
  April 7–9, 2025*. A public RBI press release (3 pages). It overlaps the
  `india-macroeconomy/` set: it states real GDP growth of 6.5% for 2024-25
  and a 6.5% projection for 2025-26, so uploading it produces new
  cross-document relations against claims already in the knowledge layer.
